# Austin Pounder research website

An individual academic research website presenting Austin Pounder's experimental and computational chemistry work.

## Audience and purpose
Researchers, potential collaborators, students, and academic readers should understand the research focus, find published evidence, and reach the researcher or his professional profiles.

## Main paths
The homepage introduces the researcher and features recent research with a graphical abstract and article link for every displayed paper. Research areas explain the scientific questions and methods and link to related publications. The publication archive supports status, topic, and text filtering. Presentations provide real PDF previews and full-resolution documents. Resources collect existing research and laboratory references. The navigation also provides the CV and email contact.

## Content constraints
Use supplied biography, affiliation, publication metadata, graphical abstracts, portrait, and presentation PDFs. Do not invent affiliations, findings, collaborations, testimonials, or publication claims. In-preparation papers are excluded from the public data and interface; submitted manuscripts remain clearly labeled.

## Offline review and publishing
The offline review artifact includes self-hosted fonts, images, PDFs, and a frozen publication snapshot. The published site loads the editable publication JSON. External articles and resources require internet access. Molecular diagrams are labeled schematic offline; live models depend on external structure providers in the publishing version. No commit, push, or deployment forms part of this review request.

## Design and accessibility
The confirmed reference is The Matter Lab at matter.toronto.edu. The refinement uses a deep green photographic introduction, strong navigation and headings, generous spacing, white reading surfaces, and serif scientific titles. Preserve readable contrast, mobile layouts, visible keyboard focus, search recovery, and reduced-motion support.

Molecular structures remain visible by default on desktop and mobile. Automatic rotation is disabled on every device.

The archive always lists all submitted manuscripts before published papers, with newest-first order within each status. Status and year groups remain separate so submitted manuscripts stay above every published paper.

The light theme uses pure white reading backgrounds. Research navigation uses text and fine rules, without decorative science icons, repeated topic captions, or slogan headings. Supplied scientific imagery remains intact.
